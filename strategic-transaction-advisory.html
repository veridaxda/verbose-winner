Advisory practices/Strategy & transactions

Strategic & Transaction Advisory

Sponsors, owners and investors need a defensible view of the economics, risks and work still required before a commitment. We bring commercial analysis, financial modelling and diligence coordination together around the decision at hand.

Enquire about this practice →

When to engage us

Energy and industrial project sponsors, corporate owners and investors

  • You are evaluating an acquisition, partnership or investment and need to validate the commercial assumptions behind the opportunity.
  • You are preparing a project for financing discussions and need a credible model, a clear funding requirement and an organised set of supporting documents.
  • Several advisers are contributing to a transaction and you need a single view of the open diligence issues, decisions and dependencies.

What we do

Strategic options and commercial diligence

Assess the market, revenue model, key contracts and operational dependencies. Test which assumptions are supported and identify gaps for specialist review.

Business planning and financial modelling

Build or review operating and cash flow models, test scenarios and identify the main drivers of value, downside and funding needs.

Financing readiness and capital structure

Assess capital requirements, compare indicative funding structures and organise the model, data room and materials for financing discussions.

Transaction coordination

Manage the commercial workstream, coordinate inputs from client-appointed advisers and keep the diligence tracker and decision timetable current.

Where a mandate requires regulated placement or other regulated services, those activities are handled by appropriately authorised providers under separate arrangements.

What you receive

We agree the outputs before work starts. Depending on the scope, they can include:

  • A commercial diligence report with risks and open questions
  • An integrated financial model with scenarios and sensitivities
  • A financing readiness assessment and data room index
  • A transaction workplan with decision milestones

Illustrative engagement

Getting a project ready for financing discussions

A sponsor has a project concept and preliminary economics. A readiness engagement can test the commercial assumptions, organise the evidence behind the funding requirement and prioritise the technical and legal work still needed.
