Working with Veridax

A defined scope and direct involvement throughout the assignment.

Start with the decision

We agree the question, deliverables, responsibilities and decision dates with your team before the engagement starts.

Make the assumptions visible

Our work distinguishes supported findings from assumptions and unresolved questions. We show what changes the recommendation and what needs further diligence.

Bring in specialists where needed

We work with your existing teams and coordinate inputs from technical, legal and financial specialists where the assignment calls for them.

Agree how to move forward

We translate findings into actions, milestones and owners. If the scope includes implementation support, we help coordinate those actions with your team.

Ways to engage

Focused assessment

A defined review for a specific decision, such as comparing supply routes, testing project readiness or assessing a market.

Project workstream

Support for a specific phase of work, such as preparing a procurement package, coordinating diligence or building an entry plan.

Ongoing advisory support

Agreed support across a series of milestones, with clear responsibilities and regular reviews of what needs to move forward.

Discuss a mandate

Tell us what you need to decide, the market or project involved, and your timing.
