Advisory practices/Infrastructure & projects

Energy Infrastructure & Project Advisory

Energy infrastructure projects bring together commercial, technical and delivery decisions. We help sponsors and operators assess readiness, structure procurement and coordinate the work needed to move projects forward.

Enquire about this practice →

When to engage us

Project sponsors, asset operators, infrastructure developers and industrial businesses

  • You are considering a terminal, storage or other infrastructure project and need to test throughput, customer demand or the operating model before committing capital.
  • You need to turn a project scope into procurement packages and compare vendor proposals on a consistent commercial basis.
  • Delivery is underway and the team needs a clearer view of milestones, contractor performance, interfaces and decisions still open.

What we do

Project readiness and commercial viability

Review demand, throughput, tariffs, commercial agreements and the main cost and schedule assumptions. Identify what needs to be resolved before the next commitment.

Procurement and bid evaluation

Define commercial scopes and evaluation criteria, structure tender packages and compare contractor or supplier proposals on cost, risk and delivery.

Contractor and supplier performance

Set up practical performance measures and milestone reporting. Track interfaces, obligations and issues for escalation.

Implementation support

Coordinate agreed workstreams with client teams and specialists. Keep decisions, dependencies and ownership visible through delivery.

What you receive

We agree the outputs before work starts. Depending on the scope, they can include:

  • A readiness assessment with open decisions and dependencies
  • Procurement packages and a bid comparison
  • A contractor performance and milestone tracker
  • A commercial action plan for the next phase

Illustrative engagement

Preparing a storage project for procurement

A developer considering a storage expansion needs to establish credible throughput assumptions and define the scope bidders should price. The work can align commercial requirements with technical inputs and give the sponsor a consistent basis for evaluating bids.
